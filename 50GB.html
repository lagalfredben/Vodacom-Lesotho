Ke a le babatsa, ،

O na le tshwanelo ya ho fumana Data ea mahala ea 50GB Moputso oa Sehopotso o sebetsa matsatsi a 30 Latela litaelo tse ka tlase ho fumana data ea hau ea 50GB.
Ho ka tsoeloa pele joang?


1. E arolelane le dihlopha tse 5 kapa metswalle e 15 ho WhatsApp (Tobetsa letshwao la "SHARE" le ka tlase).

2. O tla fetisetsoa ka boiketsetso leqepheng la "ACTIVATION" hang ha bare e tala ea netefatso e se e tletse.
